01 // HOME SCREEN

The Rotary Dial

The home screen is the instrument face. Everything you need to start a session is here — no navigation required.

SELECTING A PRESET

Rotate the dial by dragging — your presets sit as points around the outer ring. The selected preset name appears in the centre pill. The leaf icon shifts colour with the time of day: gold at dawn, blue midday, purple at dusk.

  • Rotate — drag anywhere on the dial to cycle through presets
  • Tap centre — starts the session immediately
  • Breathing ring — a slow pulsing ring confirms the dial is ready
  • State colour — leaf icon reflects time of day as a circadian cue
  • Quick edit — long press the center to edit selected preset directly

RECENT SESSIONS

The six most recent sessions appear as circles below the dial. Hold any circle for three seconds to re-launch that exact session. A circular progress arc counts down during the hold.

  • Completed circles — teal tonal, show minutes and timestamp
  • Incomplete circles — grey, show fraction of intended duration
  • Hold to relaunch — 3-second press; arc animates around the circle
  • Calm score badge — colour-coded by score band when available
  • Session details — tapping on session circle opens the session details
02 // PRESETS

Session Presets

Save your most-used configurations — duration, bells, intervals, and ambient sound — as named presets accessible directly on the dial.

WHAT A PRESET STORES

  • Session name — up to 10 characters, shown on the dial ring
  • Duration — hours and minutes, or open-ended (∞)
  • Prep time — countdown before meditation begins (0–60s)
  • Start bell — Tibetan bowl, singing bowl, high chime, or silent
  • End bell — same options; fires 10 seconds before time is up
  • Interval chime — off, or every 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 minutes
  • Ambient sound — rain, ocean, pink noise, white noise, or none
  • Ambient volume — 0–100%, saved per preset independently
MANAGING PRESETS

Presets are managed from the Presets section in the Settings page. Add, edit, reorder, or delete from one screen.

03 // ACTIVE SESSION

Running a Session

Once started, MTimer steps aside. The timer screen shows only what you need — and disappears entirely in Zen mode.

TIMER MODES

  • Fixed session — counts down from preset duration; bell fires at completion
  • Open session (∞) — counts up with no fixed end; long press Stop to close
  • Zen mode — distraction-free black screen kept awake as a focal point; toggle at the top to reveal controls
  • Prep countdown — short countdown before meditation begins, if configured
  • Interval bells — fire at configured intervals throughout the session
  • Ambient fade — soundscape fades over 8 seconds as the end bell rings

INFINITY SESSION ∞

The guarded ∞ button in the top-right of the home screen starts a session with no fixed duration. A safety guard prevents accidental activation.

  • Swipe up on the guard to arm; 3-second auto-disarm if not tapped
  • Long press the ∞ button to configure its bells and prep time
  • Long press Stop (1.5 seconds) to end the session intentionally
04 // PHYSIOLOGICAL INSIGHT

Session Calm Score

After each session, MTimer reads heart rate data from your wearable via Health Connect and calculates a settling score entirely on your device. Nothing is transmitted externally.

HOW THE SCORE IS CALCULATED

Three components, each normalised to your own physiology — not a population average. The score refines as your personal baseline builds across sessions.

  • HR Drop (40pts) — heart rate fall from session start to end
  • HR Stability (40pts) — rhythm regularity vs your personal baseline
  • Duration (20pts) — proportional reward for longer sessions
  • Calibrates after 3 sessions — refines over your next 10
  • Opt-in only — off by default; enable in Settings → Insights
  • On-device only — no data leaves the app, ever
● ENERGY — 0–39

Higher tension. The body was working to settle. Common in busy or caffeine-affected sessions.

● FOCUS — 40–59

Moderate settling. The nervous system was engaged but not fully at rest.

● AWARENESS — 60–74

Good settling. Heart rate dropped clearly and rhythm was stable.

● INSIGHT — 75–89

Deep settling. Strong parasympathetic response. Body and mind genuinely at rest.

● PRESENCE — 90–100

Exceptional settling. Rare. Heart rate dropped significantly and held stable throughout.

06 // PRACTICE GUIDE

Learn to Meditate

Seven methods drawn from classical traditions — written for clarity, suitable for any level. Accessible from the Learn tab in the navigation bar.

● BEGINNER

Watching the Breath

Notice the breath without changing it. The foundation of all other practices and the most reliable object of attention.

● BEGINNER

Hum-Sau Mantra

Sri M's instruction — Hum on the inhale, Sau on the exhale. The breath carries the mantra; no effort required.

● BEGINNER

Steady Gazing

Fix the gaze on a candle flame or point. Outer stillness produces inner stillness. Builds one-pointed concentration.

● INTERMEDIATE

So-Ham with Pathway

The breath mantra moves with a felt inner pathway through the body. So on the inhale, Ham on the exhale.

● INTERMEDIATE

Self-Inquiry

Ramana Maharshi's method. "Who am I?" — not a question to answer but a direction to follow inward toward the source of thought.

● INTERMEDIATE

Inner Sound

Nada Yoga from the Nath tradition. Block the outer ears and listen inward — from gross sounds toward the most subtle.

● ADVANCED

Inner Concentration

Attention directed toward the Ajna centre — the point between the brows. Requires stable concentration built through earlier practices.

NAVIGATION

Each method opens as an accordion. Use the jump chips at the top of the Learn screen — BASICS, METHODS, PRINCIPLES, SOURCES — to navigate directly to any section. Only one section is open at a time to maintain focus.
