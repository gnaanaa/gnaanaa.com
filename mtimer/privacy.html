Privacy Policy for MTimer

Last updated: June 7, 2026

MTimer is built as a Private-by-Design instrument. We do not operate servers, and we do not collect, store, or share your personal data.

1. Google Drive Data

MTimer uses the drive.appdata scope to allow you to backup your meditation presets and history. This data is stored in a hidden folder on your personal Google Drive. The developer of MTimer cannot access this data.

2. Health Data

MTimer accesses heart rate samples via Health Connect solely to calculate your Session Calm Score. This calculation happens entirely on your device. This data is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play Services for sign-in and backup. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle your account data.
