PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: APRIL 22, 2026

MTimer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. MTimer is designed as a minimalist, private-by-design instrument. All data created in the app is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to enable optional cloud features. We do not operate any servers and do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers of our own.

1. HEALTH CONNECT INTEGRATION

MTimer integrates with Android Health Connect to sync meditation sessions and physiological data with other health apps on your device. Our use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Accessed:

  • Write: Mindfulness Session records (start/end time, duration, and title).
  • Read: Mindfulness Session records (to display weekly totals).
  • Read: Heart Rate records (to compute the Session Calm Score).

How We Use Biometric Data:

  • We write meditation sessions to Health Connect so other apps can include them in your health summary.
  • We read Heart Rate samples solely to help you understand your body’s relaxation response. This calculation happens entirely on your device.
  • Data Sharing: We do not share, sell, or disclose your Health Connect data to any third party, advertiser, or external server.

2. GOOGLE DRIVE CLOUD BACKUP

If enabled, MTimer uses your Google Drive App Data folder to store backups of presets and history.

  • Scope: We access only the hidden App Data folder assigned to MTimer. We cannot see or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Purpose: To allow you to restore your configurations if you change devices.
  • Revocation: You can disconnect Drive at any time via the app settings or your Google Account security dashboard.

3. LOCAL DATA & SECURITY

All meditation sessions, presets, and settings are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you enable Cloud Backup. All data is protected by Android’s application sandboxing. We do not transmit your data over any network of our own.

4. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We prioritize zero-tracking. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or account credentials.
  • Location data or device identifiers for advertising.
  • Usage analytics or automated crash reports sent to our servers.

5. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

You have total control over your data:

  • Local Deletion: Clear the app’s data in Android Settings to wipe all local records.
  • Cloud Deletion: Disconnect MTimer from your Google Account to remove access to the hidden Drive App Data folder.

6. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

MTimer is designed for practitioners of all ages. Because we do not operate external servers and do not collect any personal information (PII), we do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data through a third-party service like Google Drive, please contact us immediately so we can facilitate the removal of that access.
